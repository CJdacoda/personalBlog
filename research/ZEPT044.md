# ZEPT-044: Spatial noise, identifiability and differential sensing
Review research note, 2026-11-21. Analytic benchmark and synthetic CPU simulation, not a new physical discovery.

Let s be the normalized centered linear spatial signal profile over eight sensors and b the all-ones profile. Observations y = A s + Z(b + εs) + ξ, with Z standard Gaussian, ξ independent Gaussian with coordinate variance 0.04. Unit gain w·s=1. Baseline differential w=s cancels b. Its variance is 0.04+ε², and Gaussian phase coherence is exp(-variance/2). Optimal weights for known positive definite C are C^{-1}s/(sᵀ C^{-1}s). If noise is exactly parallel to the target, no unit-gain linear estimator can remove it. A second cause model with a shared historical latent Gaussian instead of contemporaneous common Gaussian gives the same marginal distribution by construction. Snapshot data cannot identify the cause. This is not evidence of cosmic ancestry or a human quantum field.

Executed lab: 10/10 tests, 21 mismatch values, 100000 independent realizations per setting (2.1 million total), three estimators compared on shared draws. At ε=0 differential theoretical variance 0.04, phase coherence 0.98019867; sample coherence 0.98012456. Single endpoint sensor variance 3.5657143, coherence 0.1681570. At ε=1 differential variance 1.04, coherence 0.59452055; oracle calibrated covariance variance 0.04497512, coherence 0.97776340. Max absolute MC vs theoretical coherence gap 0.00545259. Oracle assumes exact covariance, not demonstrated operational calibration. No quantum hardware or experimental data. Reproduction code here is a minimal implementation of the central calculation; full test suite and sweep are in the separate chat archive.
