Can a sensor separate the signal from noise?

Eight normalized sensors; a unit-gain differential estimate cancels a uniform disturbance. If the disturbance acquires a target-shaped component ε, its residual phase variance is 0.04 + ε².

98.02%

C = exp[-(0.04 + ε²)/2]

Normalized Gaussian phase statistic, not a medical measurement or physical quantum-device benchmark. Ten unit tests passed and 21 synthetic configurations were examined. Distinct latent causes with identical probability distributions cannot be distinguished by these measurements alone.
